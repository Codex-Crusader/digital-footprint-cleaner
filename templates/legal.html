What the law gives you

GDPR (EU / UK)
Article 17, the "right to erasure", lets you require a controller to delete personal data about you. They generally have one month to respond.
CCPA / CPRA (California)
A right to know what is collected, a right to delete it, and a right to opt out of its sale. Data brokers must honour a verified request.
India, IT Act 2000 & DPDP Act
Provides for correction and erasure of personal data held by a data fiduciary.

You do not have to live in one of these places for a request to be worth sending. Most large brokers operate one removal process globally because running several is more expensive than running the strictest one.

Transparency and consent

This application only queries publicly accessible search results to help you locate your own digital footprint. Petitions are generated from the published privacy and takedown policies of the respective platforms.

Use it on your own footprint, or on someone else's with their consent. The narrowing details it accepts — employer, relatives, age — make it materially better at picking one individual out of a crowd, and that is exactly why it should not be pointed at a stranger.

What is stored, and where

  • Search results live in this server's memory for 30 minutes, then expire. They are never written to disk.
  • The removal tracker is a local SQLite file on this machine. Nothing in it is transmitted anywhere, and one button deletes all of it.
  • Your session cookie holds an opaque token and a CSRF token. No personal details are placed in it.

Liability disclaimer

Use of this tool does not guarantee removal of content. It is your responsibility to check accuracy before submitting a petition. This tool is a facilitator and makes no legally binding representation on your behalf.
